### Tips for Recording & Submission

1. **Audio Quality:** Use a clip-on lavalier mic or a good USB headset. Judges immediately disqualify or skip videos with muffled or echoey audio.
2. **Screen Recording:** Use [OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com/) (free) to record your screen at 1080p 60fps when showcasing the terminal execution and benchmark graphs.
3. **Pacing:** Practice speaking along with a phone stopwatch set to 1 minute 28 seconds. Do not rush; speak with deliberate scientific authority.
4. **Resolution & Aspect Ratio:** Export in MP4 format, 1920x1080 (16:9 widescreen), under 100 MB.
